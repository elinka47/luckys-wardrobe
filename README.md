
Lucky's Wardobe
=======

Wardrobe is a mod designed to bring more personality, style, and immersion to your Minecraft world. 
It introduces a wide variety of cosmetic clothing items—from cozy winter coats and scarves that offer protection in cold environments (with compatibility for temperature-based mods), to aprons, hats, and more. 
While these items enhance your character’s appearance, many also offer practical functionality. I aimed to stay true to Minecraft’s iconic visual style, expanding it in a way that feels natural and immersive, helping players express themselves like never before.
